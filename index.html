FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about QR codes and QRNAVI.

What is a QR code? +

A QR code is a two-dimensional code that can store information such as a website address, text, contact details or WiFi connection information.

Can I create a QR code for free? +

QRNAVI is designed to provide free QR code creation tools for the supported QR types available on the site.

Can I customize my QR code? +

Yes. QRNAVI's planned customization editor supports options such as colors, patterns, templates and other supported design settings.

How can I scan a QR code? +

Open the QRNAVI QR Scanner, allow camera access when requested, and point the camera at the QR code.

Can I download my QR code? +

Yes. The QR generator is planned to provide PNG downloads using the current QR content and customization settings.

Does a static QR code expire? +

A static QR code stores its information directly in the code. QRNAVI's V1 focuses on static QR generation; dynamic QR features may be added separately in the future.
